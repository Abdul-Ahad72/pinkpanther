Manufacturing & Processing

How Himalayan Pink Salt is Made in Pakistan

Unlike chemically processed white salt, authentic Pakistani pink salt is produced using traditional artisanal extraction and sterile food-grade mechanical milling.

Step 01
⛏️

Traditional Mining & Extraction

Extracted deep inside the subterranean tunnels of Khewra using the historic 'Room and Pillar' architectural method to ensure zero ecological collapse. Hand-selected without explosive chemical contamination.

✓ Hand-chiseled boulder blocks ✓ Zero explosive chemicals
Step 02
🔍

Optical & Manual Color Grading

Raw salt rocks are transported to processing units and sorted into distinct grades: Light Rose Pink, Medium Translucent Pink, and Deep Ruby Red depending on the natural iron & trace mineral concentration.

✓ 3 Color Grade separations ✓ Optical contaminant screening
Step 03
💧

High-Pressure Wash & Sun Drying

Selected crystal boulders undergo high-pressure purified brine washing to remove surface rock dust, followed by natural sun and hot-air desiccation tunnels to preserve mineral potency without bleaching.

✓ Pure brine washing system ✓ 100% Chemical-free drying
Step 04
⚙️

Stainless Steel Milling & Sieving

Crushed in surgical-grade SS-316 stainless steel hammer mills to prevent metal residue. Precision vibratory screens classify granules into exact international mesh sizes: Fine, Medium, Coarse, and Chunks.

✓ 0.2mm to 6.0mm granulometry ✓ Rare-earth magnetic metal separators
Step 05
🪓

Master Artisan Carving

For non-edible lines (Salt Lamps, Cooking Slabs, and Sauna Bricks), skilled Punjabi craftsmen hand-carve solid crystal boulders using diamond-tipped saws and custom lathe machines into geometric shapes.

✓ Hand-carved Natural Lamps ✓ Polished 8"x8" Gourmet Cooking Blocks
Step 06
📦

HACCP Packaging & Global Export

Packed under strict ISO 22000 cleanroom standards. Available in bulk 25kg/50kg woven PP bags, 1-ton jumbo tote bags, or custom private-label retail glass grinders and stand-up pouches for overseas retail chains.

✓ Moisture-barrier hermetic seals ✓ Worldwide shipping via Karachi Port
Geological Heritage

The Khewra Salt Mines, Pakistan

Located in Jhelum District, Punjab, Pakistan, Khewra is the world's second-largest salt mine. Discovered by Alexander the Great's army in 326 BC when horses were noticed licking the crystalline rocks, Khewra contains an estimated 600 million tons of pure fossil sea salt.

📍 Location: Jhelum, Punjab, PK
⛰️ Altitude: 288 meters
💎 Reserve: 600M+ Metric Tons
🛡️ Protected by Pakistan GI Tag

Official Geographical Indication (GI)

In 2021, the Government of Pakistan officially registered Himalayan Pink Salt under Geographical Indication (GI) laws to curb counterfeit claims and preserve the national heritage of Pakistani miners.

✓ Certified 100% Origin of Pakistan
