How Himalayan Pink Salt is Made in Pakistan

Unlike chemically stripped white table salt, authentic Pakistani pink salt is produced using traditional artisanal extraction and surgical stainless steel milling.

Step 01
⛏️

Traditional Mining & Extraction

Extracted deep inside subterranean tunnels of Khewra using the historic 'Room and Pillar' method to protect mine stability. Hand-selected without toxic blasting residue.

✓ Hand-chiseled boulder blocks ✓ Zero explosive contamination
Step 02
🔍

Optical & Manual Color Grading

Raw salt rocks are transported to state-of-the-art sorting facilities and separated into Light Rose Pink, Medium Translucent Pink, and Deep Ruby Red crystals based on iron density.

✓ 3 distinct color grade selections ✓ Optical laser contaminant screening
Step 03
💧

High-Pressure Wash & Solar Desiccation

Boulders undergo high-pressure purified brine washing to remove surface rock dust, followed by natural sun drying and hot-air desiccation tunnels without bleaching chemicals.

✓ Purified saturated brine rinse ✓ 100% Bleach-free drying
Step 04
⚙️

SS-316 Stainless Steel Milling

Crushed in food-grade SS-316 stainless steel hammer mills to prevent metallic wear. Vibratory screens classify granules into exact mesh granulometries (0.2mm to 6mm).

✓ Fine, Coarse, & Extra-Coarse sizing ✓ Neodymium magnetic metal traps
Step 05
🪓

Master Artisan Carving

For wellness and culinary slabs (Lamps, Gourmet Cooking Blocks, Sauna Tiles), Punjabi craftsmen hand-carve solid crystal boulders using diamond saws and woodcraft lathe bases.

✓ Hand-carved Negative Ion Lamps ✓ Searing Grilling Blocks (450°F Heat Safe)
Step 06
📦

HACCP Packaging & Export Delivery

Packed under strict ISO 22000 cleanroom protocol into 25kg PP bags, 1-ton bulk tote bags, or custom private-label retail grinders and zipper pouches for worldwide shipment.

✓ Food-grade hermetic moisture barrier ✓ Worldwide dispatch via Port Qasim / Karachi
Geological Heritage

The Khewra Salt Mines, Pakistan

Located in Jhelum District, Punjab, Pakistan, Khewra is the world's second-largest salt mine. Discovered by Alexander the Great's army in 326 BC when cavalry horses were seen licking the crystalline rocks, Khewra holds over 600 million tons of prehistoric fossil sea salt.

📍 Location: Jhelum, Punjab, PK
⛰️ Altitude: 288 meters above sea level
💎 Reserve: 600M+ Metric Tons
🛡️ Protected by Pakistan GI Law
